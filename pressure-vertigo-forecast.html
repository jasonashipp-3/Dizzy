      The risk levels are estimates built from the rate of pressure change, a pattern many people with vestibular conditions report. They aren't a diagnosis. Thresholds vary from person to person, so set your sensitivity and use the symptom log to tune them. The tips are general wellness guidance, not medical advice. Follow your ENT or neurotologist's plan. Call 911 if vertigo comes with sudden weakness, numbness, slurred speech, facial droop, double vision, or the worst headache of your life.
